# CpR — Cost per Resolution (HELIOS)

Retired: the earlier "Correct Prediction Rate" definition on this page is withdrawn (S0.7, 2026-10-24).

CpR is the five-component cost of one incident (token, infrastructure, storage, human time, opportunity) under the frozen price book, analysed as log(CpR). The single definition, with HR@k, Avg@5, MRR, time to correct diagnosis and the strata, is in `docs/tracking/metric_contract.md`.
