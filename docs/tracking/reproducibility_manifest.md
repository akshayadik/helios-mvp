## Section 6 — Deviation Log `commit_sha` Convention

All 18 entries in `deviation_log.jsonl` carry `commit_sha: "LOCAL"`. This is because the
deviation log CLI (`bin/log_deviation.py`) was run locally before pushing commits to the remote
repository. The HMAC-SHA256 chain is cryptographically valid; the `commit_sha` field does not
participate in the signed payload.

**Reconstruction:** To associate a deviation entry with its actual commit SHA, cross-reference
`timestamp_utc` in the JSONL entry with `git log --format="%H %ai %s"` output. Each deviation
was logged immediately before or after the corresponding commit, so timestamps differ by at most
a few minutes.
